# Progress records

- [STATUS.md](STATUS.md): current focus, immediate next task, and blockers.
- [projects/](projects/): cumulative evidence and remaining work per project.
- [sessions/](sessions/): dated history of tested work and machine handoffs.
- [templates/session.md](templates/session.md): copy for a new session.

Update notes after meaningful work and commit them with the corresponding code.
Keep the top-level status short enough to read at the start of every session.

Track two separate dimensions:

1. **Implementation:** not started, in progress, paused, or complete against the
   roadmap's exit criteria.
2. **Understanding:** unseen, explained, assisted, independent, or independently
   transferred. Attach evidence to claims; use “not assessed” where evidence is absent.

For performance experiments, record the workload, measurement boundary, prediction,
controls, environment, repetitions, correctness checks, observations, and uncertainty.
Store small summaries here; full traces and checkpoints belong in external storage.
